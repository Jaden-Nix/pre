Predora Social Feed: Feature Upgrade Plan
Phase 1: The "Essentials" (Do these first)
These are the features users expect from any modern app.
1. Edit & Delete:
• Logic: Only the author (or you, the Admin) can see these buttons.
• Visual: If a post is edited, add a tiny (edited) gray text next to the timestamp.
2. Threaded Replies:
• Logic: Don't just make it a flat list. Allow users to reply to a specific comment.
• Design: Indent replies slightly so it looks like a conversation (like Reddit or Twitter).
3. Rich Media (Images/GIFs):
• Why: Crypto culture IS meme culture. A feed with only text is boring.
• Easy Win: Add a simple "Upload Image" button. If you can't build a full GIF picker yet, just let people upload memes from their phone gallery.
Phase 2: The "Trust" Layer (Crucial for Betting)
This is what separates "Predora" from "Twitter". On Twitter, anyone can lie. On Predora, the blockchain proves the truth.
1. "Skin in the Game" Badge (The Anti-Shill Feature):
• Concept: If a user posts about "ETH hitting $5k", your system checks their wallet.
• Visual: If they actually have an open bet on that market, put a ✅ Verified Holder or ✅ Bet Placed badge on their post.
• Impact: This proves they put their money where their mouth is. Judges LOVE this.
2. User Rank / Reputation:
• Visual: Next to "Alice Founder", show her Global Rank (e.g., 🏆 Rank #4 or 🐋 Whale).
• Impact: When a "Rank #1" user speaks, everyone listens (and copies their bets).
Phase 3: The "Hype" Layer (Viral Growth)
1. The "P/L Flex" Card:
• Concept: When a user wins a bet, give them a button that says "Share to Feed".
• Visual: It automatically creates a post that says "I just made +400% on the Election Market!" with a cool green graphic.
• Impact: This triggers greed/FOMO in other users and makes them want to bet too.
2. "Bet on This" Button (The God Tier Feature):
• Recap: As discussed, allow users to attach a specific market to their post so others can bet instantly. This is your revenue generator.
