1. AI-Powered Market Creation (Instant Auto-Generation)

How it works:
	•	User enters a topic (“Will Nigeria beat Congo?”, “Will ETH hit $5k?”, “Will Drake drop this year?”)
	•	AI engine generates:
	•	The full title
	•	Market description
	•	Context summary
	•	Tags
	•	Suggested odds
	•	Recommended resolution date
	•	Suggested category
	•	User only taps “Create”.

Modes:
	1.	Binary AI → Yes/No markets (full auto)
	2.	Multiple Choice (3–6 options) → AI suggests options, but user edits manually
	3.	Traditional AMM → AI suggests initial odds based on historical patterns

This makes Predora the easiest market creation flow in the world.

⸻

2. Two Market Types

A) No-Loss Fixed Pot Markets🛡️
	•	Users deposit into a yield protocol (Aave, Compound, Lido, Yearn, Curve, etc.)
	•	The yield becomes the prize pool
	•	Principal is returned to users after resolution
	•	Winners get the interest
	•	Losers get their principal back
	•	Perfect for new users and Gen Z who fear losing money.

B) Traditional AMM Markets🔥
	•	Creator seeds liquidity (min $1,000)
	•	Odds move with trades
	•	Users profit directly from price movements
	•	Stakers can earn % of trading fees
	•	High-volume / pro-trader mode
	•	Enables whale capital + market depth
