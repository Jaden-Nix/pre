Here is exactly what you should prioritize to pass the "Technical Screening":
1. The "Must Have" (On-Chain)
You MUST deploy a simple contract to BSC Testnet that handles the basic "Bet" action.
• Why: If a judge connects their wallet and clicks "Place Bet," Metamask must pop up and ask to sign a transaction on BSC. If it doesn't, they will think you faked the whole thing.
• What to deploy: A simple Betting.sol contract. It doesn't need the complex "Swarm" logic inside. It just needs to accept funds and emit an event like BetPlaced(user, amount, prediction).
2. The "Nice to Have" (Mock/Frontend)
• Staking & Yield: Keep this mocked.
• Why: Writing a secure yield-bearing vault (like PoolTogether) takes weeks to debug.
• How to fake it: On your frontend, just hardcode the APY display (e.g., "Current APY: 12.5%"). When they click "Stake," show a success animation and update their "Staked Balance" in the local state. Do not try to integrate with Aave/Compound/Venus on testnet right now.
• Swarm Debate: Keep the debate logic off-chain.
• The debate happens in your backend (Python/Node).
• The result of the debate is just a number sent to the blockchain. You don't need the agents literally paying gas fees to talk to each other on-chain.
3. The Strategy: "Wizard of Oz"
Judges judge based on User Experience and Core Innovation, not your backend code quality.
• The Demo Flow:
1. User connects wallet (Real).
2. User sees "ETH will hit 5k" market (Real data from API).
3. User clicks "Bet No-Loss" (Real transaction on BSC Testnet).
4. The "Magic": User sees "Agents Verifying..." animation. (This can be a simple timeout or a backend call).
5. User sees "Agents Reached Consensus: 99% Confidence" (Real output from your AI script).
Summary:
• Deploy: Only the basic "deposit/bet" contract.
• Mock: Staking pools, yield generation, and complex governance.
• Showcase: The AI Agents. Even if they are running locally on your laptop, record a video of them debating. That is enough to prove the tech works.
